5 — Bounded Handoff Tasks

Each task below is scoped for a contributor who has read this report and the framework documents. No renegotiation of existing terms is required. Each task has a clear input, defined output, and a hard deadline tied to the milestone timeline above.

HANDOFF-01 · Response deadline: April 21, 2026
Meta Platform Policy Research: Web Bundle Eligibility for UEFN/VR Charity Activations
Studio B wants to sell a charity bundle ($10.99–$14.99, outfit + gadget + coins) directly via their website rather than through the Meta platform store, to avoid Meta's ~30% cut. The question is whether Meta's developer policies permit external web sales of items tied to a Meta platform VR experience, and whether a charity use case creates any policy exceptions or requires additional disclosures.
Inputs available
Studio B platform: Meta Quest / VR storefront
Bundle contents: in-game outfit, gadget(s), in-platform coins
Relevant Meta policy domains: Meta Quest Developer Policy, Meta Platform Terms, Monetization Policies
Charity framing: proceeds to St. Jude Children's Research Hospital, publicly declared
Output: Written policy summary (500–800 words) citing specific Meta policy sections. Conclusion: permitted / not permitted / requires Meta approval. Delivered to FTR Partnership Lead by April 21.
HANDOFF-02 · Response deadline: May 1, 2026
Patient Art Monetization Protocol: Consent and Permission Framework for Studio B
Studio B's activation requires St. Jude patient artwork to be adapted into in-game gadget designs and sold as part of a real-money bundle. A documented protocol must exist covering: how patients (and guardians) provide informed consent, what "express permission to sell in-game" means legally, how proceeds are attributed back to St. Jude, and what disclosures are required in the game UI and marketing materials. This protocol must be reviewed by St. Jude legal before any patient art is solicited or used.
Inputs available
SOW-001 Section 1.6 (Physical Merchandise Consultation) and Section 4 (Brand Approval Process) as the contractual anchor
Activation scope: patient doodles/art adapted into gadget designs, sold in $10.99–$14.99 bundle
Revenue flow: Digital Goods sales, proceeds to St. Jude after FTR 12.5% commission
Analogous frameworks: St. Jude's existing patient content use policies (request from St. Jude POC)
Output: Protocol draft (800–1,200 words) covering consent process, permission language, attribution requirements, and required UI disclosures. Delivered to St. Jude legal and FTR by May 1 for joint review.
HANDOFF-03 · Response deadline: June 1, 2026
Studio B Community Contest: Rules, Moderation, and Integration Specification
Studio B will run a community creation contest where players submit gadget ideas that may be selected for the patient-art-inspired in-game items. The contest must be fully specified before creative production begins at M4. The spec must cover: submission format, eligibility, judging criteria (combining community votes + St. Jude patient art team input), prize or recognition structure, content moderation rules, disqualification conditions, and technical integration requirements for the Studio B platform.
Inputs available
Studio B platform: VR, player-generated content ecosystem with established community moderation tools
Patient art protocol (output of HANDOFF-02) — must be complete before this task begins
SOW-001 Section 1.3 (Community Events) and Section 4 (Brand Approval) as the contractual scope
Campaign window: Contest runs through November 2026; winning designs must be production-ready by September 1 for integration into M5 asset submission
Output: Contest specification document (600–1,000 words) covering all above elements. Must be approved by Studio B technical lead and St. Jude POC before June 15. Delivered to FTR Partnership Lead for submission by June 1.
